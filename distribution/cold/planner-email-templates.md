# Planner Cold Email Templates

Six variants for outreach to wedding planners. Written for the secondary ICP in `zz/info/uvp-icp-etc.md` — planners specializing in destination, luxury, or design-forward weddings who influence 3–10 wedding purchases per year.

**Rules for all six:**
- Subject line under 55 characters, lowercase, no clickbait.
- First line is *never* about us. Always about them or a specific thing they made.
- One ask per email. No calendar link in the first send.
- Sign off with first name only. Wepho URL under the sig, not linked mid-body.
- Never say "quick question" or "hope this finds you well."

Replace `{{planner_first}}`, `{{studio}}`, `{{specific_wedding_or_post}}`, `{{city}}` before sending.

---

## 1. Cold intro — destination / luxury planner

**Subject:** something for the reception, not the planning

The couple keeps the output — video messages, an advice book, an emotion graph of the day.

If it's useful I'd send over two 90-second walkthroughs of past builds. Worth a look?
