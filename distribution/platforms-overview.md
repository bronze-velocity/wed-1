# Distribution Platforms — Comprehensive Overview (2026)

A long-form scan of platforms worth considering for getting eyeballs to a website or web app. For each: what content works, how organic reach behaves, how non-followers discover you, paid options, signup/verification friction, and whether links are `dofollow` (good for SEO backlinks) or `nofollow`/`ugc`/`sponsored`.

**Backlink shorthand:** `dofollow` = passes SEO authority. `nofollow` = doesn't (still useful for referral traffic and brand mentions Google can read). Most UGC platforms are `nofollow` by default in 2026; a few surprising ones still pass link equity from profile fields or specific placements.

---

## 1. Visual / Inspiration Platforms

### Pinterest
- **Content types:** Pins (single image + link), Idea Pins (multi-page, video-friendly, no external link on native pins in feed), video pins, Collections, Boards. Vertical 2:3 (1000×1500) dominates.
- **Organic reaction:** Slow-burn engagement — a Pin can drive clicks 6–18 months after posting. Saves > repins > click-throughs. Users are in *planning mode*, not scroll-entertainment mode, so intent is unusually high.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Almost entirely via search + the home feed's interest graph. Following barely matters — Pinterest is essentially a visual search engine. Keyword-rich title, description, alt text, and board name are the whole game.
- **Paid:** Pinterest Ads (Promoted Pins, Idea Ads, Shopping, Quiz Ads). CPMs cheap vs. Meta; conversion tag + catalog feed available. Minimums low.
- **Signup:** Free personal + free Business account. Business unlocks analytics + Rich Pins + ads. No verification needed to post; domain claim via HTML tag/DNS to get Rich Pins and analytics attribution.
- **Backlinks:** Pin outbound links are `nofollow`. Profile website field is `nofollow`. Still valuable for referral traffic.

### Instagram
- **Content types:** Reels (dominant), Carousels (highest save rate), single photos, Stories (24hr), Lives, Broadcast Channels, Notes.
- **Organic reaction:** Reels get the discovery lift; carousels get saves and shares (educational/utility). Stories are for existing followers only. Comment velocity in the first 60 min matters heavily for Reels ranking.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Reels tab, Explore, hashtag/audio/location pages, "suggested for you" in feed. Follower count matters less than watch-through + shares + saves. Audio choice is a real ranking lever.
- **Paid:** Meta Ads Manager (shared with Facebook). Advantage+ campaigns are the default in 2026. Lookalike, interest, retargeting via Pixel/CAPI. Boost button is a trap — use Ads Manager.
- **Signup:** Free. Meta Verified ($15/mo) gives priority support + badge, does not boost reach much. Business/Creator accounts free — required for insights + ads.
- **Backlinks:** Bio link is `nofollow`. In-post links do not exist (text links aren't clickable). Story link stickers `nofollow`.

### TikTok
- **Content types:** Short-form video (7s–3min sweet spot for reach; long-form up to 60min exists but underperforms), Photo Carousels (Slideshows), Live, Stories.
- **Organic reaction:** The FYP is the great equalizer — a brand-new account with zero followers can hit millions of views. Completion rate + rewatch are the top signals. Comments and shares > likes.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Nearly 100%. Following is almost irrelevant to reach. Sound, hashtags, on-screen text, and first-frame hook drive placement.
- **Paid:** TikTok Ads Manager. Spark Ads (boost existing organic posts, best CTR), In-Feed, TopView, Branded Hashtag. Spark Ads generally the best ROI.
- **Signup:** Free. Business account free, gives analytics + ads. No verification to post; verified badge is application-based and rare.
- **Backlinks:** Bio link `nofollow`. In-video links don't exist in the traditional sense.

### YouTube
- **Content types:** Long-form (>8min is monetization sweet spot), Shorts (<60s, separate algorithm), Live, Community posts, Playlists.
- **Organic reaction:** Long-form videos compound for years (evergreen search). Shorts get burst views but rarely convert to subs or clicks. Playlists heavily boost session watch time.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** YouTube Search (2nd largest search engine on earth), Suggested Videos (right rail / next-up), Home feed, Shorts feed. CTR on thumbnail + first 30s retention are the two dominant ranking signals.
- **Paid:** Google Ads (YouTube). Skippable in-stream, non-skippable, Bumper, Discovery, Shorts ads, Masthead. Cheapest premium video inventory on the internet by CPM.
- **Signup:** Free. Channel verification (phone) unlocks custom thumbnails + >15min uploads. YPP (monetization) requires 1000 subs + 4000 watch hrs or 10M Shorts views + 90 days.
- **Backlinks:** Video description links are `nofollow`. Channel "links" section `nofollow`. Community posts `nofollow`. Still a massive referral driver.

## 2. Long-Form Writing / Publishing

### Medium
- **Content types:** Articles (2–8 min read sweet spot), lists, personal essays, tech tutorials. Publications (multi-author) still exist and matter for distribution.
- **Organic reaction:** Members-only stories get algorithmic boost + earn writer payouts via reading time. Non-members-only posts get less push. Follower count matters more than on TikTok/IG — but a good title can still hit For You feeds cold.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** For You feed (interest-based), Topic pages, Publications, Google search (Medium ranks well). Boosted status (editorial pick) is the biggest single lift.
- **Paid:** No native ad platform for creators. Some publications offer sponsored slots privately.
- **Signup:** Free reader account. $5/mo Medium Membership required to unlock paywalled reading + monetize your own writing via Partner Program.
- **Backlinks:** Links inside articles: `nofollow` for most, but Medium historically has passed some equity via crawlable content and canonical import (`Import a story` feature lets you canonical-tag back to your own site — genuinely useful SEO play).

### Substack
- **Content types:** Email newsletters (primary), Notes (Twitter-like short posts, growing fast in 2026), Podcasts, Video, Chat threads.
- **Organic reaction:** Email is 1:1 — no algorithm between you and readers. Notes has an algorithmic feed and is now a real discovery surface.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Notes feed, Substack recommendations (other writers recommend you — the #1 growth channel on Substack), Featured lists, Substack Search, Google. Cross-recommendations are the killer feature.
- **Paid:** No native ads. Substack Boost (recently launched) lets writers pay to appear in Notes-adjacent surfaces.
- **Signup:** Free to create publication + write. Stripe connect for paid subs (10% platform fee). No verification.
- **Backlinks:** Post body links `dofollow` (unusual and valuable — Substack is a legit backlink source). About page + custom domain also `dofollow`.

### LinkedIn
- **Content types:** Text posts (surprisingly best for reach), Documents (carousel PDFs — huge dwell), Articles (long-form, poor reach), Newsletters (subscriber-based, high reach), Video (growing), Polls, Live.
- **Organic reaction:** Dwell time + comments (esp. thoughtful replies you engage with) drive reach. Outbound links suppress reach — post native + drop link in first comment is the standard workaround.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** "People you may know", feed suggestions from second-degree network, hashtag follows, Search, Company Page follows spill into employee posts.
- **Paid:** LinkedIn Ads (Campaign Manager). Highest B2B CPMs on the internet ($30–150+ CPM) but unmatched targeting by job title, company size, seniority.
- **Signup:** Free personal + free Company Page. Profile completeness unlocks reach. LinkedIn Premium ($40–120/mo) doesn't boost reach but unlocks InMail + Sales Nav insights.
- **Backlinks:** All external links `nofollow` — in posts, articles, profile "Website" field, Company Page. Zero SEO juice, meaningful referral traffic.

### Dev.to
- **Content types:** Technical articles, tutorials, discussions, series.
- **Organic reaction:** Warm dev community, decent comment culture. Reactions ("hearts", "unicorns", "bookmarks") stack for ranking.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Tag feeds, homepage listing, weekly digest email, Google (Dev.to ranks well for niche dev queries).
- **Paid:** Sponsor slots and some brand tags — modest program.
- **Signup:** Free, GitHub OAuth typical. No verification.
- **Backlinks:** Canonical URL support (declare `canonical_url:` in frontmatter — republish articles pointing back to your blog for SEO). In-body links `dofollow` historically, though this has fluctuated. Canonical support is the real prize.

### Hashnode
- **Content types:** Dev blogging platform with a native custom-domain feature (your blog lives at your subdomain but is hosted by Hashnode).
- **Organic reaction:** Smaller than Dev.to but growing. Feed + weekly digest.
- **Discovery:** Feed, tags, homepage featured, some Google.
- **Paid:** N/A meaningful.
- **Signup:** Free.
- **Backlinks:** Custom-domain feature preserves your SEO. In-body article links `dofollow` and crawled.

## 3. Community / Discussion Platforms

### Reddit
- **Content types:** Text posts, link posts, images, videos, polls. Subreddit-specific rules dominate everything.
- **Organic reaction:** Ruthless. Self-promotional posts get downvoted or removed. The 9:1 rule (nine helpful comments per one self-promo post) is real. But a genuinely useful post on the right subreddit can drive tens of thousands of visitors overnight and rank on Google for years.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** r/all, subreddit hot/rising, Reddit search, and — increasingly critical — Google. Since 2024, Reddit content is a top-3 result for most informational queries in the U.S. because of the Google-Reddit content deal.
- **Paid:** Reddit Ads (Promoted Posts, Conversation Ads, Video). Cheap CPMs. Interest + subreddit targeting.
- **Signup:** Free. Karma + account age gate posting in many subs (some require 100+ karma or 30+ days). Email verification recommended. No ID verification.
- **Backlinks:** All submitted links + comment links `nofollow ugc`. Reddit itself ranks in Google — you get *indirect* SEO benefit when your brand/URL appears in a highly-ranked Reddit thread.

### Quora
- **Content types:** Question answers, Spaces (topic communities), long-form.
- **Organic reaction:** Long tail — a good answer can compound for years via Google. Native distribution is weaker than it was.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Google search (Quora ranks well), topic feed, Quora Digest email.
- **Paid:** Quora Ads (text ads, image ads, promoted answers). Underused; cheap in most verticals.
- **Signup:** Free, real-name required per TOS.
- **Backlinks:** In-answer external links `nofollow`. Profile links `nofollow`. Referral traffic + brand mention only.

### Hacker News
- **Content types:** Link submissions + text posts (Ask HN, Show HN).
- **Organic reaction:** Highly technical, allergic to marketing language. Show HN posts with a working demo and honest post-mortem tend to perform. A front-page hit = 20k–100k visitors in 24hrs.
- **Discovery:** New page → front page via early upvotes (first hour is everything). No follower graph.
- **Paid:** None. There's a "Who's Hiring" thread monthly — that's it.
- **Signup:** Free. Karma unlocks features (downvote, flag). No verification.
- **Backlinks:** `nofollow` on all links. HN threads themselves rank in Google — indirect benefit.

### Indie Hackers
- **Content types:** Milestone posts, product launches, discussion threads, Groups.
- **Organic reaction:** Supportive founder community. Traction stories perform.
- **Discovery:** Homepage feed, Groups, weekly digest email.
- **Paid:** No ad system.
- **Signup:** Free.
- **Backlinks:** Profile has a Website field — historically `dofollow` on some sections (worth checking with a live inspection before quoting). Post links typically `nofollow`.

### Discord
- **Content types:** Chat messages, threads, forum channels, voice/stage, announcements.
- **Organic reaction:** Depends entirely on the server. Requires being a known participant before you can drop links.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Discord Discovery (server directory), Server Listing sites, invite links shared elsewhere.
- **Paid:** No ad platform. Boosts pay for server perks, not reach.
- **Signup:** Free, phone verification often required to post in larger servers.
- **Backlinks:** N/A (content not crawled).

### Slack communities
- **Content types:** Same as Discord — messages, threads, DMs.
- **Discovery:** Invite-only or application-based mostly (Rands Leadership, Online Geniuses, Women in Product, etc.).
- **Backlinks:** N/A.

### Telegram
- **Content types:** Channels (broadcast), Groups (chat), Stories, Bots.
- **Organic reaction:** Channel subscribers see everything (no algo). Groups depend on member activity.
- **Discovery:** Directory sites, cross-promotion, shared invite links. Native discovery is weak.
- **Paid:** Telegram Ads (min. €2M spend at launch, now down to ~$500 for smaller advertisers via resellers). Sponsored messages in large channels.
- **Signup:** Free, phone required.
- **Backlinks:** N/A.

### WhatsApp
- **Content types:** Channels (broadcast, launched 2023), Communities, Groups, Status.
- **Organic reaction:** Channels: subscribers see all. No public discovery beyond WhatsApp's directory.
- **Discovery:** WhatsApp channel directory (by country + category), invite links.
- **Paid:** WhatsApp Business API for messaging (not ads to non-customers). Meta ads that click-to-WhatsApp are the paid entry point.
- **Signup:** Free, phone number.
- **Backlinks:** N/A.

## 4. Micro-blogging / Short-Form Text

### X (Twitter)
- **Content types:** Tweets, threads, images, video (up to 4hr for premium), Spaces (audio), Communities, Articles (long-form), Polls.
- **Organic reaction:** For You feed heavily favors X Premium subscribers + high-engagement replies. Native content (no external links) reaches ~5–10x more than posts with links.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** For You algorithmic feed, Explore, Trends, Communities, Search.
- **Paid:** X Ads (Promoted posts, Video Ads, Takeover). Effectiveness varies wildly since 2023 — cheap CPMs but questionable audience quality in some segments.
- **Signup:** Free. X Premium ($8–16–22/mo tiers) required to unlock verification checkmark + reach boost + longer posts + edit.
- **Backlinks:** All in-tweet links `nofollow`. Profile URL `nofollow`. Referral only.

### Bluesky
- **Content types:** Skeets (300 char), images, video (short), Starter Packs, custom feeds.
- **Organic reaction:** Chronological or custom-feed based — no black-box algorithm. Discovery is harder than X but community is engaged.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Custom Feeds (community-built topical feeds — huge growth channel), Starter Packs, Reposts, quote-posts.
- **Paid:** None yet.
- **Signup:** Free, email + phone verification for signup. No ID.
- **Backlinks:** Post links `nofollow`. Profile website `nofollow`.

### Threads
- **Content types:** Text posts (500 char), images, video, Instagram cross-posting.
- **Organic reaction:** Algorithmic For You feed, heavy suggestion of accounts you don't follow. Reach can be surprisingly high on Threads still (Meta pushing growth).
- **Discovery:** For You feed, Search, cross-promotion from IG follows.
- **Paid:** Ads rolled out 2025 — buy via Meta Ads Manager.
- **Signup:** Requires Instagram account.
- **Backlinks:** All external links `nofollow`.

### Mastodon
- **Content types:** Toots, images, video, polls. Federated across instances.
- **Organic reaction:** Chronological, small but loyal audiences. Tech/journalist/academic-heavy.
- **Discovery from non-followers:** Boosts (retweets), hashtags, federated timeline, Trunk (opt-in directory).
- **Paid:** None.
- **Signup:** Free, choose an instance. No verification, but rel="me" link-back verification badge is popular.
- **Backlinks:** Toot links `nofollow`. Profile links `nofollow` — BUT rel="me" verification with your own site is a nice trust signal (Google reads it).

### Farcaster
- **Content types:** Casts (320 char), Frames (interactive mini-apps embedded in feed), Channels.
- **Organic reaction:** Small (~200k daily) but engaged crypto/builder audience. Frames are a distinctive discovery tool.
- **Discovery:** Channels (topical), Trending, algorithmic feeds via Warpcast client.
- **Paid:** No formal ad system yet; sponsorship of casts happens informally.
- **Signup:** ~$5 one-time onboarding fee (funds an on-chain account). Wallet required.
- **Backlinks:** Links in casts `nofollow`.

## 5. Q&A / Reference / Wikis

### Stack Overflow / Stack Exchange
- **Content types:** Questions + answers.
- **Organic reaction:** Merit-based. Self-promo is aggressively removed.
- **Discovery:** Google (dominant), site search, tag feeds.
- **Paid:** Stack Ads (small B2B/dev-focused platform).
- **Signup:** Free. Reputation gates most privileges. Email verify.
- **Backlinks:** Post links `nofollow` unless you have 20+ rep on some sub-sites (rare). Profile "About me" links `nofollow` in most cases but crawled.

### Wikipedia
- **Content types:** Encyclopedia articles.
- **Organic reaction:** Zero tolerance for self-promotional editing. Requires notability + independent citations.
- **Discovery:** Google (top-3 for most named-entity queries).
- **Paid:** N/A.
- **Signup:** Free. Anonymous edits allowed on many pages.
- **Backlinks:** External links `nofollow`. Still massively valuable for brand credibility + citations sometimes lift you in Google's Knowledge Panel.

### Product Hunt
- **Content types:** Product launches, Ship (waitlist), Discussions, Collections.
- **Organic reaction:** Launch-day is the whole game. Top-5 finish → 10–50k visitors + press pickup. Building the audience *before* launch (Ship, follow, teaser) drives ranking.
- **Discovery:** Homepage daily leaderboard, category pages, weekly/monthly leaderboards, email digest.
- **Paid:** Sponsored slots (~$500–3000/day depending on placement).
- **Signup:** Free. Account age + karma matter for upvote weighting.
- **Backlinks:** Product-page links `dofollow` historically — worth verifying at launch time. Genuinely valuable SEO backlink from a high-authority domain.

### G2 / Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice / TrustRadius
- **Content types:** Product listings + reviews (SaaS/B2B).
- **Organic reaction:** Buyer-intent traffic. Reviews drive rank on-platform.
- **Discovery:** Google (dominant), category pages, comparisons.
- **Paid:** Yes — pay-per-click bidding for category placement, Sponsored badges, lead-gen.
- **Signup:** Free listing + claimed profile. Reviewer identity verification (LinkedIn/work email).
- **Backlinks:** Product profile links usually `nofollow`. Referral + intent traffic is the value.

## 6. Video / Streaming

### Twitch
- **Content types:** Live streams, VODs, Clips.
- **Organic reaction:** Discovery is hard — small streams stay small unless raided or hosted. Category "browse" is main entry for cold viewers.
- **Discovery:** Directory browsing, raids, recommendations, YouTube clips.
- **Paid:** Twitch Ads (mostly self-serve for larger campaigns via Amazon Ads).
- **Signup:** Free. Phone verify to chat in many streams. Affiliate/Partner tiers unlock monetization.
- **Backlinks:** Panel links `nofollow`. About-page links `nofollow`.

### Vimeo
- **Content types:** Video hosting, portfolios.
- **Organic reaction:** Not really a discovery platform anymore — it's a hosting/embed utility.
- **Discovery:** Staff Picks, categories.
- **Paid:** Paid plans required for most business features. No ad platform.
- **Signup:** Free tier + paid tiers.
- **Backlinks:** Video description links: historically some `dofollow`, worth checking. Portfolio site with custom domain is best play.

### Rumble / Kick
- **Content types:** Long-form video / live streaming (right-leaning creators + gaming respectively).
- **Discovery:** Homepage, trending, subscriptions.
- **Paid:** Small ad platforms.
- **Signup:** Free.
- **Backlinks:** Description links typically `nofollow`.

## 7. Audio

### Spotify (Podcasters)
- **Content types:** Podcasts (via Spotify for Creators / RSS ingest), Video Podcasts, Music (artists via distributors).
- **Organic reaction:** In-app search + recommendations. Video podcasts get boosted.
- **Discovery:** Home, Browse, Search, "You Might Like".
- **Paid:** Spotify Ad Studio (audio + video). Podcast host-read sponsorships via Megaphone/others.
- **Signup:** Free for listeners, Spotify for Creators is free. Music requires a distributor (DistroKid, TuneCore).
- **Backlinks:** Show notes may support links but they render inside the app — `nofollow`. Your Spotify show page can be Googled but doesn't pass link equity to your site.

### Apple Podcasts
- **Content types:** RSS-ingested podcasts.
- **Discovery:** Charts (category ranking = big deal), Search, Editorial.
- **Paid:** Apple Podcasts Ads (rolling out to more markets).
- **Signup:** Free with Apple ID. Manual review before first show is approved.
- **Backlinks:** N/A meaningfully.

## 8. E-commerce / Marketplace / Bookings

### Etsy
- **Content types:** Digital + physical product listings (crafts, printables, wedding suites).
- **Discovery:** Etsy search (huge intent), Etsy Ads, external Google.
- **Paid:** Etsy Ads (on-site) + Offsite Ads (external ad spend Etsy runs on your behalf, taking commission).
- **Signup:** Free shop, one-time $15 setup fee in some regions, listing fees ($0.20/listing/4mo). Bank + tax info required.
- **Backlinks:** Shop page + About links `nofollow`. Referral traffic if visitors click through.

### Amazon
- **Content types:** Product listings (KDP for books, Merch for print-on-demand, main store for physical).
- **Discovery:** Amazon search, A9 algorithm, Sponsored placements.
- **Paid:** Amazon Ads (Sponsored Products, Brands, Display).
- **Signup:** Seller Central requires verification (ID + bank + tax).
- **Backlinks:** No external links from listings; brand storefronts limited internal linking.

### Shopify (own store) + collectives
- Not a discovery platform itself, but the Shop app has a discovery feed for stores. Worth listing.

### The Knot / WeddingWire / Zola
- **Content types:** Vendor listings + reviews (wedding vendors).
- **Discovery:** Category + geo search — where couples actively shop.
- **Paid:** Featured vendor slots, storefront upgrades ($100–2000+/mo).
- **Signup:** Vendor account with business info verification.
- **Backlinks:** Vendor storefront often has a `nofollow` website link. Some tiers may unlock `dofollow` — verify before pricing decisions.

## 9. Design / Portfolio

### Behance / Dribbble
- **Content types:** Design projects, case studies, shots.
- **Discovery:** Curated feeds, categories, Adobe's cross-promotion (Behance).
- **Paid:** N/A for organic; Adobe promotes some via Behance-Live etc.
- **Signup:** Free (Adobe or Dribbble account).
- **Backlinks:** Behance profile links `nofollow`. Dribbble profile links `nofollow`. Both drive real client-facing traffic though.

### Awwwards / SiteInspire / Land-book / Godly / Muzli
- **Content types:** Website submissions (for design showcase).
- **Discovery:** Design-crowd traffic, weekly emails.
- **Paid:** Awwwards charges submission fees ($40–120). Others vary.
- **Signup:** Free profile, paid submissions on some.
- **Backlinks:** Award-directory backlinks are often `dofollow` — a real SEO win, especially Awwwards + SiteInspire. Verify per site.

## 10. Aggregators / Curators / Newsletters

### Newsletter sponsorships (Beehiiv, Substack, ConvertKit creators)
- **Content:** Paid mentions in curated newsletters (industry-specific).
- **Discovery for you:** Whatever the newsletter reaches — direct email opens.
- **Paid:** Sponsorships $50–5000+ per send.
- **Backlinks:** Depends on newsletter's site. Often `nofollow`, sometimes `dofollow` if the sponsor is featured on a public web archive.

### Beehiiv Boosts
- Newsletter creators pay each other for cross-recommendations (like Substack Recommends but paid). Effective for growing a list you own.

### "Best of X" list sites (Toolify, TheresAnAIForThat, Futurepedia, AlternativeTo)
- **Discovery:** Category browsers + Google. TAAFT + Futurepedia drove huge referral traffic during the AI-tools boom; still meaningful.
- **Paid:** Featured / sponsored slots.
- **Signup:** Submit tool for free, verification not required, some paid tiers.
- **Backlinks:** Often `dofollow` on directory profile pages — genuine SEO backlinks. Worth submitting to 30–50 relevant directories.

### AppSumo / DealMirror / Prime deals
- **Content:** Lifetime-deal marketplaces (SaaS mostly).
- **Discovery:** Huge deal-hunter audience.
- **Paid:** Revenue share.
- **Backlinks:** Product listing `dofollow` in many cases.

## 12. Underrated / Long-Tail

### GitHub
- **Content:** Repos, READMEs, Discussions, Gists, Pages (free static hosting).
- **Discovery:** Trending, Explore, Google. A well-README'd repo can rank on Google and drive real traffic.
- **Signup:** Free.
- **Backlinks:** README external links `dofollow`. Profile links `dofollow`. GitHub is a *legitimate* backlink source with high domain authority.

### Notion (public pages) / Gumroad / Beacons / Linktree
- Discovery: Minimal native, but ranks in Google.
- **Backlinks:** Beacons/Linktree bio-link tool pages typically `nofollow`. Notion public pages `nofollow`. Gumroad product pages `nofollow`.

### Are.na
- Curated visual mood-boarding, small but design-heavy audience.
- **Backlinks:** Block links `nofollow`, but content is Google-indexed.

### Flickr / 500px
- Photography-first. Discovery via Explore, groups, tags.
- **Backlinks:** Description links `nofollow`.

### SlideShare (LinkedIn) / Speaker Deck
- Slide deck hosting. Long-tail Google traffic if titled well.
- **Backlinks:** Speaker Deck links `nofollow`. SlideShare varies.

### Google Business Profile
- For local businesses. Discovery via Google Maps + local pack search.
- **Signup:** Free, requires postcard/phone/video verification.
- **Backlinks:** Website field `nofollow` but *massive* SEO signal (NAP consistency, Maps ranking, local pack).

## Cross-Cutting Takeaways

**Backlinks that actually pass SEO equity (worth pursuing deliberately):**
- Substack post bodies
- Dev.to / Hashnode (canonical support + often `dofollow`)
- Product Hunt product pages
- GitHub READMEs + profiles
- AI/tool directories (TAAFT, Futurepedia, Toolify, AlternativeTo)
- Awwwards + design-showcase directories
- Medium (via canonical import for your own domain)

**Platforms where the discovery mechanic completely ignores your follower count:**
- TikTok (FYP is king)
- Pinterest (search + interest graph)
- YouTube (Suggested + Search)
- Reddit (subreddit hot)
- Hacker News (front-page voting)
- Google itself

**Platforms where follower count matters most:**
- Instagram Stories (followers-only)
- LinkedIn (network + second-degree)
- Substack email (subscribers)
- Discord / Slack / Telegram groups (members)

**Verification friction, from lowest to highest:**
- No verification: Reddit, Mastodon, Bluesky, HN, Dev.to, Hashnode, most dev/design directories
- Email/phone: X, TikTok, IG, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads
- Real identity or business docs: Google Business, Nextdoor, LinkedIn (soft), Etsy, Amazon, Meta Verified
- Payment / wallet: Farcaster ($5), Substack (Stripe), Medium ($5/mo for monetization)

**The 2026 shift to internalize:** Google is now downranking pure SEO farms in favor of Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Substack content. Posting on those platforms is often the fastest way to appear on the first page of Google for a given query — even if the platforms themselves give you `nofollow` links. The Google visibility is the prize; the direct backlink is the bonus.
