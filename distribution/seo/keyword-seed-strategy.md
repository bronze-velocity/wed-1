# Keyword Seed Strategy

Seeds live in `keyword-seeds.json` (327 terms). These feed Phase 2 of `keyword-research-plan.md` — paste them into DataForSEO Labs to expand to thousands of candidates with real volume data.

## How the list was built

**Six categories, different logic for each:**

- `reception_entertainment` / `game_specific` / `custom_personalized` / `planner` — intent-mapped seeds covering the core Wepho audience. Game-specific terms (trivia, bingo, shoe game, roast) are individually winnable because no single incumbent owns them.
- `long_tail_creative` — phrased the way frustrated couples actually type: "games that aren't corny", "entertainment besides dancing", "how to get guests off their phones". Low volume but high conversion intent; also useful as blog H2s and FAQ questions that feed AI Overviews.
- `german` / `german_high_volume` — DE/AT/CH expansion seeds. Only worth running through Labs if that market is in scope.
- `high_volume` — shorter head terms (wedding games, reception ideas, photo booth alternatives) added for breadth. Most won't be directly rankable early but Labs will surface the winnable long-tail variations hanging off them.

**Competitor brand terms** (kahoot, slido, mentimeter + wedding) are intentional — searchers using those terms already want interactive wedding tech and are the warmest possible audience.

**Photo booth adjacent terms** (360 booth, mirror booth, selfie station) are included because Wepho's positioning wedge is "the personal alternative to a photo booth" — ranking alongside those terms puts us in front of buyers at the right moment.
