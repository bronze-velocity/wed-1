# Keyword Seed Strategy

Seeds live in `keyword-seeds.json` (327 terms). These feed Phase 2 of `keyword-research-plan.md` — paste them into DataForSEO Labs to expand to thousands of candidates with real volume data.

## Positioning context

Wepho sits at the custom end of a spectrum where every competitor (Kahoot, Google Forms, Joy, photo sharing apps) sits at the generic end. The category doesn't have a name yet — we're creating it. That shapes what keywords matter: couples don't search "custom wedding app" because the category isn't in their heads yet. They search for the problem (bored guests, generic reception, nothing to do at cocktail hour) or for the generic tools they've heard of. We meet them there.

## How the list was built

**Six categories, different logic for each:**

- `reception_entertainment` / `game_specific` / `custom_personalized` / `planner` — intent-mapped seeds covering the core Wepho audience. Game-specific terms (trivia, bingo, roast, shoe game) are individually winnable because no single incumbent owns them, and each maps to a real Wepho app type.
- `long_tail_creative` — phrased the way frustrated couples actually type: "games that aren't corny", "entertainment besides dancing", "how to get guests off their phones". Low volume but high conversion intent; also useful as blog H2s and FAQ questions that feed AI Overviews.
- `german` / `german_high_volume` — DE/AT/CH expansion seeds. Only worth running through Labs if that market is in scope.
- `high_volume` — shorter head terms added for breadth. Most won't be directly rankable early but Labs will surface the winnable long-tail variations hanging off them.

**Competitor brand terms** (kahoot, slido, mentimeter + wedding) are intentional — the one-pager's key objection is "Is this just Kahoot?" which means that's exactly the comparison in buyers' heads. Searchers using those terms want interactive wedding tech and are the warmest possible audience.

**Photo booth adjacent terms** (360 booth, mirror booth, selfie station) are included as a tactical SEO play: `strategy.md` identifies the photo booth as the incumbent reception activity in the same budget slot. Ranking alongside those terms puts Wepho in front of buyers mid-consideration. This is an SEO tactic, not the core positioning.
