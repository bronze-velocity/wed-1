# DataForSEO — Key Conclusions

*Synthesized from the keyword enrichment run (327 seeds → 100 shortlisted, 100–30K vol/mo) and the AI probe data. US market unless noted.*

---

## Keyword landscape

- **Top volume term:** "wedding guestbook" — 18,100/mo, HIGH competition, $3.29 CPC. Biggest single prize; `/apps/video-guestbook` must own it.
- **Second tier (8,100/mo each):** newlywed game questions, wedding photo booth, wedding favor ideas, party games for adults / adult party games. All actionable but via blog, not existing routes.
- **Game/entertainment cluster dominates:** the highest-volume winnable keywords are all guest-facing game queries — couple quiz, newlywed game, wedding shoe game, wedding games. Maps cleanly to Wepho's app catalog.
- **Commercial-intent keywords are sparse and competitive:** "wedding app" (1,300/mo, $3.63 CPC), "wedding quiz" (720/mo, $4.40 CPC), "wedding cocktail hour entertainment" (140/mo, $14.39 CPC — highest CPC in the shortlist). Few but valuable.
- **Most game-specific terms are LOW or MEDIUM competition** — winnable early without DA authority.

## Existing routes vs. content gaps

- **41 / 100 keywords map to existing pages** — those routes can be optimized now without new builds.
- **59 keywords need new pages** — almost all are blog posts, not additional app/product pages.

## Competitor & tactical observations

- **Photo booth is the incumbent** in the same reception-activity budget slot; ranking alongside photo booth terms (8,100/mo, high CPC) is an SEO tactic that puts Wepho in front of buyers already mid-consideration.
- **Kahoot/Slido/Mentimeter + wedding terms** were seeded intentionally — those searchers want interactive wedding tech and are the warmest possible audience.
- The category "custom wedding app" doesn't exist in search yet — couples search for the problem or the generic tool. Meeting them at problem-awareness queries (shoe game, newlywed game, bingo) is the right angle early.

## AI / GEO monitoring

- One DataForSEO AI probe run (7 US prompts + 2 LLM responses + 7 Google AI Mode + 5 AI keyword-volume keywords) costs **~$0.04/run**.
- Weekly cadence = ~$2–3/year. Cost is not the constraint; deciding what prompts to monitor is.
- DataForSEO AI answers "is Wepho cited in real ChatGPT/Google AI search results?" — OpenRouter can't answer that. OpenRouter is cheaper for content generation and copy stress-testing.
- Recommended stack: DataForSEO monthly cron for GEO audits (~$0.50/mo) + OpenRouter for content work (<$5/mo).
