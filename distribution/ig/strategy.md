# Instagram Strategy for Wepho

Custom wedding web apps, ~$2,000/app. Two audiences on IG: **couples** (primary) and **wedding planners** (secondary, but the highest-leverage referral channel).

Instagram is the natural home for this business — wedding discovery is visual, emotional, and already happens here. Pinterest is where couples plan; Instagram is where they get inspired and where vendors get found.

---

## The core insight

The product has a **built-in viral moment**: a room full of guests reacting to something on a big screen that they just made together. That's inherently short-video-shaped. Every real wedding we do is a Reel waiting to be captured.

If we do one thing right on IG, it's this: **capture every reception**. 30 seconds of the crowd erupting when the Love Letter reveals, or laughing at a Who Said It answer, is worth more than any produced content.

---

## Content pillars

Aim for a 4-pillar rotation so the grid doesn't feel one-note:

### 1. The Moment (Reels — 40% of output)
Real footage from real weddings. Guest phones lighting up. The big-screen reveal. Couples' reactions. Room-scale audio (cheering, laughter).
- Hook in first 1 second: the reaction shot, not the setup
- Text overlay: "This is what happens when 120 guests write love notes to the couple"
- End on the couple's face

### 2. Behind the Custom (Carousels — 25%)
Show the *custom* part. This is what separates us from generic photo-booth apps.
- Slide 1: "We built a trivia game just for Sarah & Marcus"
- Slides 2–5: actual questions from their app ("Who said 'I love you' first?")
- Last slide: the room playing it
- Save-worthy because it makes couples think *"I want that for us"*

### 3. Ideas & Inspiration (Carousels — 20%)
Broad-appeal saveable content that reaches non-followers.
- "10 reception moments guests actually remember"
- "5 alternatives to the guest book that don't get thrown away"
- "What to do during dinner so guests don't just scroll"
- These get saved and shared to partners — pure top-of-funnel

### 4. Planner-facing (Reels + Carousels — 15%)
Content that speaks to planners specifically. Post these knowing planners are watching.
- "How we work with planners" (Reel, 30s)
- "The 3 questions planners ask us most"
- Behind-the-scenes of a planning call
- Tag planners you've worked with — they'll reshare to Stories

---

## The Collab lever (most important growth tactic)

Instagram's **Collab post** feature lets a post appear on two accounts' grids simultaneously and reach both follower bases. For a wedding vendor this is the single biggest growth mechanism.

For every wedding:
1. **Tag the planner, photographer, videographer, venue, florist** in the post
2. **Propose a Collab post** with the photographer — they get to show the fun moment they captured, we get shared reach
3. **DM the planner** the raw clips so they can post them
4. **Ask the couple** if we can tag them (many say yes for the year after their wedding)

Wedding vendor accounts follow each other heavily. One good Collab with a mid-tier planner (10k followers) can outperform months of solo posting.

---

## Posting cadence

Sustainable > aggressive. Missing weeks kills momentum more than posting less.

- **3 Reels/week** (Mon, Wed, Fri)
- **2 Carousels/week** (Tue, Thu)
- **Stories daily-ish** (5–7/week) — repost tagged content, polls, "which app should we build next?"
- **1 planner-targeted post/week** minimum

Every Reel gets **cross-posted to TikTok and YouTube Shorts** the same day. Same file, same caption, zero extra work.

---

## Caption & CTA patterns

- **Keyword-loaded first line** — IG search is now text-based. "Custom wedding reception app" should appear literally.
- **Ask a question** in the caption to drive comments ("What's the moment you most want your guests to remember?")
- **Save CTA**: "Save this for your reception planning"
- **Share CTA**: "Send this to your partner"
- **DM CTA for leads**: "DM us WEDDING to see how we'd build one for you" — routes high-intent traffic into a conversation, not a form
- Link in bio → homepage (or a Linktree with `/`, `/planners`, `/apps`)

---

## Hashtags (light touch)

3–5 per post. Mix of size:
- **Big-broad** (>1M): `#weddingideas` `#weddingplanning`
- **Mid-niche** (100k–1M): `#weddingreception` `#weddingentertainment` `#uniquewedding`
- **Small-targeted** (<100k): `#modernwedding` `#weddingtech` `#interactivewedding`

Hashtags are no longer the discovery engine — they're a small boost. Don't over-invest.

## First 90 days (concrete plan)

**Weeks 1–2: Foundation**
- Set up profile, 6 launch posts (2 Reels, 2 carousels, 2 single images) so the grid isn't empty
- Follow ~200 wedding planners, photographers, and venues in target markets
- Set up Highlights with existing photography

**Weeks 3–8: Build the pattern**
- Ship 3 Reels + 2 carousels/week
- Every wedding: capture ≥3 Reel-ready clips, ship within 48h
- DM every planner whose wedding we work at, propose Collab post
- Reply to every comment within 24h

**Weeks 9–12: Compound**
- Identify top 2 planner accounts open to collab, propose a joint carousel ("5 ways to make your reception unforgettable")
- Start "app of the week" series drawing from the 20-app catalog
- Boost 1 top-performing Reel with $50 to test paid amplification to engaged-with-wedding-content audiences

## What to measure

Weekly, not daily:
- **Saves** and **shares** per post (best proxies for real interest)
- **Profile visits → website clicks** (bio link taps)
- **DM inquiries mentioning IG**
- **Follower growth from Reels vs. carousels** (tells you which format to lean into)

Ignore: likes, follower vanity count, hashtag reach.

---

## What NOT to do

- **No stock footage.** Real weddings only. Guests can smell staged content instantly.
- **No overproduced Reels.** Vertical phone footage with room audio outperforms polished edits for wedding content.
- **No pricing in captions upfront.** ~$2K is a considered purchase — get them to DM first, share the number in conversation.
- **No generic "book a call" CTAs.** "DM us your wedding date and we'll show you what we'd build" is warmer and higher-converting.
- **No cross-posting TikTok watermarks** to Reels — IG suppresses it. Export clean, then upload separately.
