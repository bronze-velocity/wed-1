## 1. Betting on the Wedding — https://www.bettingonthewedding.com

**Direct review pages:**
- [Betting on the Wedding — Reviews (own site, 4.7★)](https://www.bettingonthewedding.com/reviews) — aggregated testimonials from ~25,000+ couples (their own claim).
- [Apple App Store — Ratings & Reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/betting-on-the-wedding/id6447250181?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone) — most useful for unfiltered user feedback.
- [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.weddingbets) — user ratings + reviews.

**Summary of sentiment:**
- Positive: concept praised as fun and unique; guests enjoy the competitive element.
- Negative: the $2.99/guest join fee is not clearly advertised; paid-betting feature criticized because pledged money isn't actually collected — winners get nothing. Suggested fix from users: require buy-ins upfront.

## 3. Kululu — https://www.kululu.com/wedding-photo-sharing-app

**Direct review pages:**
- [Kululu Wall of Love (own site)](https://www.kululu.com/wall-of-love) — curated testimonials, 4.7★ / 500+ reviews claimed.
- Independent third-party reviews (competitor blogs — biased but useful):
  - [Kululu Review — joinmymoment.com](https://blog.joinmymoment.com/kululu-review-is-it-the-best-way-to-collect-wedding-and-event-photos/)
  - [Kululu Review 2026 — oureventalbum.com](https://oureventalbum.com/reviews/kululu)
  - [Kululu Review 2026 (UK angle) — gatherandgroup.com](https://www.gatherandgroup.com/blog/comparisons/kululu-review-uk-couples)
  - [12 Wedding Photo Apps Compared — easyweddingalbum.com](https://easyweddingalbum.com/blog/wedding-photo-sharing-comparison)
  - [10 Best Wedding Photo Apps — knipsmig.com](https://knipsmig.com/best/wedding-photo-apps)

**Summary of sentiment (~4.1/5 across independent reviews):**
- Positive: no-download QR-code flow drives high guest participation; slideshow loved by guests; low setup effort for couples.
- Negative: pricing feels high on tight budgets; free plan is very restrictive (100 uploads, 24h upload window, 7-day album retention, standard quality) — limits often only become obvious mid-event; occasional bugginess; less customizable than expected.

## General tips for finding more reviews

- **Reddit search:** `site:reddit.com "kululu"`, `site:reddit.com "betting on the wedding"`, `site:reddit.com "my wedding trivia"` — often the most candid feedback.
- **App stores** (for anything with an iOS/Android app): Apple App Store and Google Play reviews are the least filtered.
- **Wedding forums:** WeddingWire, The Knot community, Hitched (UK), r/weddingplanning.
- **TikTok / Instagram:** search the product name — real couples post honest post-wedding recaps.
- **Trustpilot / G2 / Capterra:** none of these three appear to have meaningful listings, but worth checking directly.
