# Wepho — Pinterest Creative Ideas

A brainstorm bank for pins, video loops, quote cards, and idea-pin sequences. Organized by format, then by angle. Every idea assumes the same north-star framing: **the phone is the input device, the room is the output** — and **specificity creates believability**.

Design defaults for all assets:
- **Static pins:** 1000×1500 (2:3). Off-white `#F7F6F3` or near-black `#111111` background, purple `#6B5CE7` accent, one photograph or one device mockup as the hero.
- **Video/idea pins:** 1080×1920 (9:16). Keep text out of the top 180px and bottom 260px. Hook in the first 2 seconds — always a visual, never a title card.
- **Type:** system-ui, oversize serif-feeling weight for headlines when possible (via weight + tracking); one line, max ~7 words.
- **Voice on every asset:** warm, specific, confident. Never "a trivia game about your relationship" — always the actual question a real couple would answer.

## 1) Static image pins (1000×1500)

### A. The "phone → big screen" reveal (workhorse format)
Split-frame vertical pins. Top two-thirds: a hand holding a phone with a Wepho screen. Bottom third: a photograph of the reception moment the phone triggered. Overlay a keyword-first headline.

- *"The guest types. The room reacts."* — phone typing a love letter, big screen showing it above the head table, grandma dabbing her eye.
- *"Every question is about them."* — phone showing "Where did they have their first kiss?" with real-couple options, big screen tallying live votes.
- *"Grandma's message. On the wall. In her handwriting."* — phone with a handwriting-style textarea, projector wall showing the message in cursive.
- *"The scavenger hunt only your guests could win."* — phone with a clue ("Find the person who introduced them"), venue photo of two guests high-fiving.
- *"A trivia round that only your people can play."* — phone showing "What did she say when he proposed?" and four believable, funny options.

### B. The "6 apps" grid pin (SEO-heavy, high save-rate)
A 2×3 grid of phone mockups, each labeled with the app name and a one-line hook. Title bar reads *"6 interactive wedding apps built for one night only."* Repeat at least 4 variations, rotating which 6 of the 20 apps are featured and rotating the keyword.

Keyword rotations to test:
- "Unique wedding reception ideas"
- "Fun things for guests to do at a wedding"
- "Interactive wedding entertainment"
- "Unforgettable wedding guest experiences"
- "Wedding big-screen moments"

### C. Price-reframe pin (the most pinnable copy asset)
Big, quiet type on off-white. Purple accent on the last line. No image.

```
Flowers: $800. Dead by morning.
Photographer: $3,500. You see it in 6 weeks.
Caterer: $5,000+. Forgotten by Tuesday.
Wepho: $2,000. They talk about it for years.
```

Alternate versions:
- *"$800 for flowers your guests won't remember. $2,000 for a moment they'll retell for a decade."*
- *"Cost of the average wedding centerpiece: $150. Cost of a wedding your guests can't stop describing: less than you think."*

### D. "The 60-second rule" explainer pin
Vertical stack of three phone mockups with numbered captions:
1. Guest scans the QR (0:05)
2. Types their message (0:30)
3. Puts the phone back down (0:55)

Headline: *"Phone in, phone out. Under 60 seconds."*
Subhead: *"Then everyone looks up."*

### E. Vibe-filter carousel stills
One pin per vibe, five total. Each is a rich photograph with a single overlay word.

- **Make them laugh** — table of guests mid-cackle at a projector reveal.
- **Make them cry** — bride reading a projected message, mascara streak, groom's hand on her back.
- **Get them talking** — two strangers at a dinner table pointing at their phones, comparing.
- **Create a keepsake** — a printed hardcover book on a kitchen table, months later, morning light.
- **Stop the room** — wide shot of every guest looking at one screen at once.

### F. "Idea → app" pins (the specificity moment)
The couple's real detail on the left, the app it became on the right. Purple arrow between.

- *"They met during a rainstorm →  A trivia app that opens with 'What were you both running from?'"*
- *"He proposed in her grandmother's kitchen →  A memory map with a pin on the kitchen and 40 guest messages layered on top."*
- *"Their first date was a disastrous escape room →  A scavenger hunt where the final clue is the venue's bar."*

### G. The 20-app "menu" pin
An off-white pin styled like a wedding menu card. Serif title *"Tonight's Experience"* with 3–5 app names in italic. Small line at the bottom: *"Built for the [Couple Name] wedding — never used again."*

### H. Quote cards (see full quote bank below in §3)
Simple, high-contrast, one line, oversized. Wepho wordmark bottom-right, small.

## 2) Short-form video pins (6–15 seconds, 9:16)

Every video opens on a **visual hook in the first 2 seconds** — no title cards. Text overlays only after the moment lands.

### The hero loop: "Love Letter Machine, 10 seconds"
- 0:00 — Close-up of a thumb typing on a phone: "The first time I saw you two together, I finally understood the word 'fit.'"
- 0:03 — Cut to the couple mid-reception, laughing at something else.
- 0:05 — The room's attention shifts. Big screen slides the message into view.
- 0:08 — Bride's hand covers her mouth. Groom leans in.
- 0:10 — Freeze on their faces. Text overlay: *"Wepho — one custom app per couple."*

### "The scan"
6-second loop. QR code on a table card → phone scans → phone unlocks a Wepho welcome screen that says *"Welcome to Emma & Jordan's night."* No cuts to the wall. Just the intimacy of the personalized handoff.

### "The reveal montage"
15 seconds. Four different weddings, four different apps, four moments of guests looking up at a projector at the same time. No voiceover. Ambient reception audio. End card: *"The phone is the input. The room is the output."*

### "Grandma"
8 seconds. Old hands typing slowly on a phone. Cut to the projector showing her message. Cut to her face watching it. End frame text: *"She's never used an app. She used this one."*

### "The 20 apps, in 12 seconds"
Idea-pin video where each of 20 phone mockups swipes past at ~0.5s each with the app name overlaid. Ends on: *"Pick one. We build it for you."*

### "What did she say when he proposed?"
Trivia app screen appears on a phone. Four multiple-choice answers, all specific, all funny (*"'Yes obviously' / 'Are you serious right now?' / 'Wait — is this real?' / She just cried"*). Whole room's votes appear on the projector. Bride, on camera, says *"It was the crying one."* — cut.

### "Room reaction cutdown"
12 seconds. No app footage at all — just guest reactions. Laughter, tears, a table pointing at the screen, a groom's dad wiping his eyes. Final frame text-only: *"We build the app. Your guests build the moment."*

### "Before / After the QR"
Split screen. Left: awkward cocktail-hour silence, two strangers not talking. Right: same two strangers, phones out, laughing at each other's answers on the icebreaker app.

### "Emotion Pulse timelapse"
Show a time-of-day emotion graph filling in over the day, from arrivals to dancing. Text overlay: *"Your wedding, as felt by your guests."*

### "The keepsake unboxing"
Slow, quiet. A hardcover book arrives in the mail three weeks after the wedding. Hands open it. Every guest message, in order, on cream paper. Last frame: *"Flowers wilt. This doesn't."*

## 4) Idea/multi-page pins (up to 20 pages, 1080×1920)

Idea pins are Pinterest's carousels — huge for saves. Format each as a mini-essay with one big visual per page and 1 short line of text.

### "5 wedding entertainment ideas your guests will actually remember"
Pages 1–5, one Wepho app each, with a specific example. Page 6: soft CTA to the homepage demo.

### "How the Love Letter Machine works" (step-by-step)
1. QR on the table card
2. Guest types a message
3. Couple (or a trusted friend) approves it
4. Message appears on the big screen with animation
5. Everyone looks up together
6. All messages become a keepsake book after the wedding

### "20 interactive wedding apps — pick one, we build it"
Rapid grid pages, four apps per page, five pages total, ending on the demo CTA.

### "Wedding reception timeline, rethought"
Cocktail hour → dinner → speeches → dancing, one app per moment, showing which Wepho app fits where. Ends: *"Which moment do you want to make unforgettable?"*

### "5 objections couples have — and the actual answer"
Straight from the objections table in the one-pager. One page per fear + answer. This is a *save-me-for-later* format for undecided couples.

### "What a $2,000 wedding line item actually looks like"
Page 1 hook: cost per hour of the day compared. Page 2–5: what the money buys, told in specific scenes. Page 6: the keepsake.

### "The 60-second rule"
One page per second bucket (0–15, 15–30, 30–45, 45–60), showing what the guest is doing. Hook: *"If it takes longer than a minute, guests don't use it."*

### "5 things the projector shows that your photographer can't capture"
Five vertical stills, each a big-screen moment. Positions Wepho as a complement to (not a competitor of) the photographer — good for cross-pinning by photographers.

## 5) Photo-shoot shot list (assets we need to create these)

If commissioning or restaging photography, prioritize in this order — every pin idea above pulls from this list:

1. **Hand + phone, portrait crop, off-white background** — the workhorse mockup base. 10+ variations of typing, tapping, scrolling.
2. **Big screen / projector reveal, wide shot** — a room full of guests, backs to camera, looking at a projected screen. Even a mocked-up screen composited in post works.
3. **Reaction close-ups** — bride's face mid-tear, groom's dad laughing, grandma smiling, a table of friends pointing.
4. **QR-code table card, styled** — sitting on a real reception place setting, next to a wine glass, in warm candlelight.
5. **The keepsake book** — a hardcover book on a kitchen table, morning light, a hand turning a page. Shot three weeks-after mood, not wedding-day mood.
6. **"After the QR" pairs** — two strangers laughing at their phones together, phones angled toward each other.
7. **Detail shots** — a phone screen through a champagne flute; a projector's light in a dim room; hands holding a phone lit only by the screen.

## 6) Board-level creative ideas

Each board is a landing zone. The board cover image and description are ranking signals — treat them like pin creative.

- **Board: Interactive Wedding Reception Ideas** — cover is the projector-reveal wide shot. Description leads with "unique wedding reception ideas, fun guest activities, big-screen moments."
- **Board: Fun Things for Wedding Guests to Do** — cover is two strangers laughing at their phones. Aimed at the couple's search intent.
- **Board: Wedding Big-Screen Moments** — cover is a bride crying at a projector. Story-first board.
- **Board: The 20 Wedding Apps** — cover is the "menu" pin. Every one of the 20 gets a pin here.
- **Board: For Wedding Planners** — cover is a clipboard + phone flat lay. Aimed at the planner ICP; different copy voice.
- **Board: Wedding Keepsakes That Aren't Photos** — cover is the hardcover book. Adjacent search intent, high save rate.
- **Board: Rethinking the Reception Timeline** — cover is a schedule graphic. Content-adjacent board that draws in planners.
