# Niche sites
  - The real primary channel is probably neither SEO nor GEO — it's the 
  wedding-vendor network (planners, photographers, videographers, DJs,  
  venue coordinators) plus inspiration platforms (Pinterest, Instagram, 
  TikTok). Bespoke wedding services propagate through referrals and    
  visual proof, not search.                                             
  - Case studies replace pillar guides. One 90-second video of your app
  running at a real wedding beats ten "how to plan a wedding" articles.
